// 07 FAQ

Before you ask

How does payment work?

Standard projects require 50% upfront and 50% upon final delivery. Matrix Elite and Overlord projects can use 40% / 30% / 30% milestones.

Do I own the code?

Yes. Final source, documentation and environment access are transferred after final payment.

Are domain and hosting included?

Domain and cloud hosting are billed to the client account, keeping full ownership and allowing clear annual renewals.
