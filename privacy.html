Privacy Notice

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This notice explains how Eltayar Group handles information submitted through the project enquiry form on this website.

Information we collect

When you submit a project enquiry, we collect the name and email address you provide, any optional phone number, your preferred contact method, the selected package, your project description, your launch-offer review preference, and your consent to be contacted.

Why we use it

We use these details to review your enquiry, understand the requested work, contact you about the project, and maintain a record of incoming requests.

Where it is processed

Submitted enquiries are sent to the Eltayar Group Supabase project through a server-side function and stored in its project-request database. Access to stored requests is restricted by the database's access policies to authorized administrator accounts. The website's public configuration contains only a publishable client key; privileged service credentials are not placed in the page.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell enquiry information. Infrastructure providers that operate the website, database, or any configured notification service may process information as needed to provide those services. We do not promise that optional email notifications are enabled for every submission.

How long information is kept

Enquiry records are retained while they are needed for business follow-up and record keeping, unless deletion is requested or a longer retention period is required by law. No fixed deletion schedule is stated here.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you submitted. Contact: nerotest6@gmail.com. We may need to verify the request before changing or deleting a record.

Contact

For privacy questions, email nerotest6@gmail.com.
