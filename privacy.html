Privacy Notice

When you submit a project request, Eltayar Group collects the name and email address you provide, and any optional phone number, preferred contact method, selected package, and project details.

Purpose

We use these details only to review your enquiry, contact you about the requested work, and manage the request.

Retention and access

Requests are stored in the project request system and are intended to be accessible only to authorized administrators. Retain records only as long as needed for the enquiry and applicable legal obligations. Configure and verify your database access policies before collecting real customer data.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit the form and contact us by email instead. To request access, correction, or deletion of a request, email nerotest6@gmail.com.

Important setup note

This notice is a starter template, not legal advice. Before launch, update it to reflect your actual business, retention period, hosting providers, and applicable law.
