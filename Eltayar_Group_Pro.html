// 06 FAQ

Before you ask

How long does a project take?

Core Launch usually ships in 1 to 2 weeks and Neon Pro in 3 to 4. Matrix Elite takes 6 to 10 weeks depending on modules. Overlord is scoped per project during the blueprint step.

Do I own the code?

Yes. You receive the full source, documentation and access to every environment when the final payment clears.

What does the cyber security work include?

Secure authentication, encrypted data, security headers, dependency and vulnerability scans on every tier. Matrix Elite adds a full audit and hardening pass. Overlord adds a penetration-test report and ongoing monitoring.

How does payment work?

50% to start the build and 50% on delivery. Larger Overlord projects can be split into milestones.
